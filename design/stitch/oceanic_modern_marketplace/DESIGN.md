## Brand & Style

This design system powers a contemporary, on-demand home services and domestic artisan marketplace engineered for modern suburban and urban hubs. Rejecting industrial tropes of caution tape, rugged hardware, and harsh construction textures, the experience embodies the fluid, high-trust precision of premier lifestyle and mobility applications.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Vetted Confidence:** High-trust patterns establish immediate peace of mind with verified artisan badges, police clearance disclosures, upfront community ratings, and protected transactional states.
- **Dynamic & Refreshing:** Driven by a vibrant cyan/ocean blue palette that communicates technological clarity, hygiene, and domestic rejuvenation.
- **Effortless & Frictionless:** Minimalist task paths with clean dual-parameter discovery (Service + Suburb/Neighborhood) and transparent pricing in South African Rand (ZAR).

### Design Movement: Modern Fluid Minimalism
The visual execution marries crisp, modern minimalism with subtle tactile depth. Pure white, airy canvas layers, and soft slate typography sit alongside vibrant cyan accents and low-opacity ambient drop shadows, delivering a responsive, mobile-first marketplace interface.

## Colors

The color system centers around a refreshing, high-contrast cyan core that provides energetic clarity across domestic service flows while maintaining pristine accessibility standards.

### Key Palettes
- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Sky / Ocean Blue):** The principal brand color. Powers primary calls-to-action, hero action buttons, active navigation states, selected service indicators, links, and high-impact UI highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0F766E` - Deep Pine / Trust Teal):** Communicates institutional reliability, secure payment processing, identity verification badges, and guarantee markers.
- **Tertiary (`#E05A12` - Warm Terracotta):** Repurposed strictly as an accent color for promotional highlights, urgency states, and local feature callouts.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` - Deep Slate):** Delivers optimal legibility across typography, iconography, and structural line-work.

### Light Mode Tokens & Contrast Architecture
- **Base Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` for primary cards, sheets, and elevated modules; `#F8FAFC` for foundational page backdrops.
- **Interactive States:** Primary buttons rest at `#0284C7`, hover to `#0369A1`, and focus with an accessible cyan focus ring (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`).
- **Surface Elevation Containers:** Low-contrast neutral surfaces (`#F1F5F9`) define disabled states, search fields, and secondary card containers.

## Typography

The typography is built entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering clean geometric proportions, welcoming rounded letterforms, and high legibility across mobile screen densities.

### Typography Hierarchy & Rules
- **Display Pricing:** The `price-display` style must be applied to all numerical rates and quote comparisons with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align prices neatly across listings.
- **Headings:** Heavy weights (`700` and `800`) are paired with tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) for crisp, modern editorial impact.
- **Body & Microcopy:** Normal weights (`400`) utilize balanced line heights (1.5x–1.55x) for comfortable reading on task descriptions, reviews, and booking confirmations.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout accommodates dual-column search patterns on mobile while expanding into rich, multi-column dashboard and directory views on larger screens.

### Grid & Responsive Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column grid, maximum layout width `1280px`, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with fluid margins between `1.5rem` and `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` internal gutters.

### Component Spacing Cadence
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-label gaps, rating pill interiors, and metadata grouping.
- `space-md` (16px): Input padding, list item separation, and button padding.
- `space-lg` (24px): Artisan card interiors, modal content padding, and filter grouping.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section layout spacing and category rows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layering system that relies on ambient shadows tinted with soft slate and low-contrast borders rather than harsh, saturated drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Background Canvas):** Pure background (`#F8FAFC`), no elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Listings):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and subtle ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Elements & Floating Controls):** Search bars, filter pill docks, and active floating menus: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Sheets):** Mobile bottom-sheets and booking modals: `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`.
- **Hover Transitions:** Interactive cards lift slightly (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) with an expanded shadow tinted with cyan: `box-shadow: 0 12px 20px -4px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The system uses a **Rounded** shape language (`level 2`) to balance contemporary consumer app ergonomics with clean visual structure.

### Radius Distribution
- **Form Controls & Triggers (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Default radius for buttons, input fields, and search segment blocks.
- **Cards & Modules (`1rem` / 16px):** Used for service cards, profile modules, and booking summary panels.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Applied to mobile bottom sheets, featured category cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved for status chips, verification tags, and price badges.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action:** Solid vibrant cyan (`#0284C7`) background, crisp white label (`#FFFFFF`), `12px` border radius, and bold `label-md` weight. Hover state shifts to deep cyan (`#0369A1`). Active press state triggers `transform: scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Action:** Ghost white/slate background (`#F1F5F9`), slate label (`#0F172A`), with a subtle border (`#E2E8F0`). On hover, shifts to `#E2E8F0`.
- **Trust / Safety CTA:** Deep teal (`#0F766E`) background with white text, used for certified security triggers, identity checks, and payment verifications.

### Dual-Input Search Bar
- **Container:** Pure white card (`#FFFFFF`) with `16px` radius, hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), and Tier 2 floating elevation.
- **Left Field (Service Query):** Clean typography with cyan focus state.
- **Divider:** Vertical hairline (`1px solid #CBD5E1`, `8px` vertical margin).
- **Right Field (Suburb / Area):** Location pin icon followed by suburb selector.
- **Search Trigger:** High-contrast `#0284C7` pill button embedded on the right.

### Artisan & Service Listing Cards
- **Container:** Pure white card (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` radius, Tier 1 elevation, and subtle hover transition.
- **Identity Header:** Artisan avatar (`48x48px`, `12px` radius) alongside name, neighborhood, and verified badge.
- **Stats Row:** Compact star rating badge paired with completed job count.
- **Footer:** Numerical pricing using `price-display` in slate (`#0F172A`) paired with a direct `#0284C7` action button.

### Form Inputs, Checkboxes & Radios
- **Inputs:** Crisp white base, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `12px` radius. Focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `6px` radius for checkboxes, `9999px` for radios. Checked state renders solid `#0284C7` fill with white indicator.

### Badges & Trust Chips
- **Verified Professional:** Pill shape, background `#ECFDF5`, label `#059669` with a solid green check icon.
- **Trade Certified:** Pill shape, background `#F0F9FF`, label `#0284C7` with a cyan shield icon.
