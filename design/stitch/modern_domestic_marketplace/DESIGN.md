## Brand & Style

This design system powers a contemporary, on-demand home services and artisan marketplace engineered for the South African suburban and urban landscape (Sandton, Fourways, Rosebank, Cape Town City Bowl, Umhlanga). It explicitly rejects the aesthetic tropes of heavy industrial construction, yellow caution tape, and rugged hardware sites. Instead, it adopts the polished, consumer-grade sophistication of premier hospitality and mobility platforms (Airbnb, Uber, Waasha).

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Vetted Confidence:** High-trust interaction models emphasize verified identities, police clearance, background checks, and community reviews.
- **Warm & Energetic:** Grounded in African earth and sunlight through terracotta and rich ember tones, balancing operational efficiency with human warmth.
- **Effortless & Frictionless:** Streamlined task flows, unambiguous pricing displayed in South African Rand (ZAR), and frictionless dual-parameter discovery (Service + Neighborhood).

### Design Movement: Modern Tactile Minimalism
The visual execution blends clean modern minimalism with subtle tactile depth. Crisp white and cool slate surfaces are layered over soft ambient drop shadows, precision hairline borders, and generous radii, delivering a tactile, responsive mobile-first experience.

## Colors

The palette balances energy, safety, and domestic calm. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E05A12` - Terracotta Ochre):** The core brand driver. Applied to primary calls-to-action, active selection rings, booking triggers, and high-impact UI highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0F766E` - Deep Sea Pine / Trust Teal):** Communicates institutional reliability, secure payment processing, identity verification badges, and guarantee markers.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Vibrant Cobalt):** Reserved for technical credentials, insurance badges, trade certification labels, and transactional notifications.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** Provides maximum legibility for typography, headers, icons, and structural divider states.

### Functional Roles & Canvas
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and elevated sheets; `#F8FAFC` for page backgrounds and underlays.
- **Muted Surfaces:** `#F1F5F9` for secondary card tiers, disabled buttons, and search container wells.
- **Verified Professional Token:** Background `#ECFDF5` with foreground `#059669`.
- **Accreditation Token:** Background `#F0F9FF` with foreground `#0284C7`.
- **Safety / Alert:** `#DC2626` for cancellations, dispute flags, and critical warnings.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric foundation, open apertures, and contemporary letterforms evoke optimism, approachability, and structural precision.

### Typographic Rules
- **Numerical Hierarchy:** Currency formats (`R450/hr`, `From R1,200`) utilize `price-display` with tabular figure styling (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure instant visual scannability across artisan quote comparisons.
- **Headlines:** Display weights (700 and 800) feature negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to command attention without visual sprawl.
- **Body & Microcopy:** Generous 1.5x to 1.55x line heights prevent visual fatigue during long service-scope reviews or Terms of Service scans.
- **Labels & Trust Badges:** Always set with a minimum medium weight (600 or 700) to ensure high visibility on tinted badge pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a structured fluid grid optimized for high-density mobile interactions and clean desktop multi-pane workflows.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Used for split-screen maps, booking confirmation flows, and merchant dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, fluid margins scaling between `1.5rem` and `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with fixed `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` internal component gutters.

### Spacing Cadence
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for internal element grouping (e.g., star icon to review score, badge icon to badge label).
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, segmented input field stacking, and button icon separation.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body padding, section divider steps, and bottom sheet containers.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for separating major homepage content modules and service categories.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layering system that avoids harsh drop shadows, choosing instead diffused warm-slate ambient shadows paired with low-contrast borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Listings):** `#FFFFFF`, hairline boundary `border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, paired with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Search & Floating Panels):** Dual-input sticky search bars and active filter bars feature: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Drawers & Bottom Sheets):** Deep mobile bottom-sheets and artisan profile previews use an intentional boundary: `box-shadow: 0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Interactive Transition:** When hovered or tapped, listing cards elevate slightly by translating `translateY(-2px)` with shadow bloom: `box-shadow: 0 12px 20px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`level 2`), conveying hospitality, accessibility, and modern mobile app ergonomics.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Input Fields & Buttons (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Used for interactive triggers, dual search pills, and form inputs.
- **Standard Service Cards (`1rem` / 16px):** The default card radius for service provider listings, service category cards, and booking summary blocks.
- **Large Cards & Modals (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Applied to desktop hero modules, category banners, bottom-sheet overlays, and onboarding panels.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved for verification badges, category tags, counter pills, and floating action indicators.

## Components

### Dual-Input Search Bar (Hero Discovery)
A signature component unifying service type and geographic location:
- **Container:** Segmented white card (`#FFFFFF`) with `16px` border-radius, `Tier 2` shadow, and `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Left Input (Service):** Clean typography with placeholder (e.g., "Plumbing, Electrical, Handyman"), paired with a subtle magnifying glass or wrench icon.
- **Divider:** Vertical 1px hair-line `#CBD5E1` (`margin: 8px 0`).
- **Right Input (Location):** Location pin icon followed by suburb selector (e.g., "Sandton, Johannesburg", "Fourways", "Bryanston").
- **CTA:** Primary terracotta pill button anchored directly inside the container (`#E05A12`), with hover expansion.

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button:** High-tactile `#E05A12` with solid white text, `12px` radius, bold label. Hover: `#C2410C`. Active: `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#F1F5F9`, slate text `#1E293B`, subtle border `#E2E8F0`. Hover: `#E2E8F0`.
- **Trust / Safety CTA:** Deep teal `#0F766E` background with white text, utilized exclusively for identity checks, safety locks, and insurance policy disclosures.

### Verification & Trust Badges
- **Verified Professional:** Pill shape (`9999px`), background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, containing an embedded solid green checkmark icon.
- **Certified & Insured:** Pill shape, background `#F0F9FF`, text `#0284C7`, with a shield or credential ribbon icon.
- **Top Rated Local:** Subtle ochre wash `#FFF7ED`, text `#C2410C`, with a star emblem.

### Service & Artisan Listing Cards
- **Structure:** `16px` radius, white base, `Tier 1` elevation.
- **Header:** Artisan avatar (`48x48px`, `12px` radius) alongside Name, Suburb, and the Verified Badge.
- **Metrics Bar:** Flex row housing rating (e.g., `★ 4.96 (124 reviews)`) and completed jobs metric (`340+ jobs in Sandton`).
- **Footer:** Fixed price presentation (e.g., `From R450` in `price-display` weight) alongside a direct "Book Now" or "View Profile" tactile trigger.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Toggle Controls
- **Form Controls:** Styled with `6px` radius for checkboxes, `9999px` for radios. Unchecked: `border: 2px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked: Solid `#E05A12` fill with crisp white vector markings.
- **Focus Rings:** Accessible outer halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(224, 90, 18, 0.25)`.
